# Assigning a contractor

**Status:** Ready. Landlord and contractor.

A landlord can put a contractor on a work order directly, without a bid. The priced path is [Proposals and award](award.md).

On the work order, the assignments block lists who is already on it, the visit time, and whether they accepted.

![Assign a contractor](images/work-order-show.png)

1. FixIt Co is already accepted on this request, scheduled for November 1.
2. **Assign a contractor** lists companies with a date field and an Assign button. **Relevant only** narrows the list to the trade on the work order. **View portfolio** opens that contractor's past work.
3. The contractor sees the job under Assigned Work. An assignment can be pending, accepted, declined, or completed.
4. Assigning someone adds them to that work order's [message thread](messages.md).

Upcoming dated visits are listed on [Schedule](schedule.md).
