# Assigning a contractor

**Status:** Ready. Landlord and contractor.

A landlord can put a contractor on a work order that already exists, without a bid. Sending a new job to someone they already work with is [Quick jobs](quick-jobs.md). The priced path is [Proposals and award](award.md).

On the work order, the assignments block lists who is already on it, the visit time, and whether they accepted. A pending assignment shows **Accept job** and **Decline job** to that contractor.

![Assign a contractor](images/work-order-show.png)

1. FixIt Co is already accepted on this request, scheduled for November 1.
2. **Assign a contractor** lists companies with a date field and an Assign button. **Relevant only** narrows the list to the trade on the work order. **View portfolio** opens that contractor's past work.
3. The contractor sees the job under Assigned Work. While it is pending they accept or decline it. After that it can be accepted, declined, or completed.
4. Assigning someone adds them to that work order's [message thread](messages.md).

Upcoming dated visits are listed on [Schedule](schedule.md).
