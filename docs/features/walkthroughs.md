# Walkthrough times

**Status:** Ready. Landlord and contractor.

Before bids, a landlord can publish times for contractors to walk the property.

On a job, open **Walkthroughs**.

![Walkthroughs](../walkthrough/06-walkthroughs.png)

1. Choose a date, a duration, a timezone, and one or more hours. Capacity is how many contractors can book the same hour.
2. Turn on “walkthrough required” and a contractor cannot submit a proposal until they have a confirmed time.
3. The card on the right is what the contractor will see, including times already published. Each published slot can be removed.
4. After publishing, the form keeps that date and duration and leaves the hours unchecked, so another slot can be added. A new job takes its timezone from the property's state.

The contractor books from the job page. One active booking per job. A full slot cannot be taken twice.

![Contractor job](../walkthrough/12-contractor-job.png)

Booked times are listed under **Walkthroughs** in the contractor sidebar. They do not appear on [Schedule](schedule.md), which is only for work-order visits.

The screenshot above was taken before the form kept the published date. On a current server the date, duration, and timezone stay filled in, and no hour box is pre-checked.
