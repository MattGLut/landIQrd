# Not built yet

These are not screens with a missing button. They are absent, so a demo does not imply them. HOA dues, violations, and architectural requests belong to neIQhbor, a separate product.

## Payments

No rent collection, no invoices, and no contractor payouts. A lease stores a monthly rent and a deposit. A proposal stores a bid, and awarding it stores that price on the work order. Paying either amount still happens outside the app. Left out of this pass on purpose.

## Plans and a public site

Visiting the site sends you to log in. There is no pricing page, no plan picker, and no subscription. Left out of this pass on purpose.

## Listings and applications

A vacant unit is a unit with no active lease. Nothing publishes a listing, a rent price for the public, or photos for prospective tenants. The way onto a unit today is a [lease invitation](lease-invitations.md) to someone the landlord already chose.

## Screening and e-sign

The landlord attaches a document on the [lease](leases.md). There is no background check, no income verification, and no signature request.

## Inspections

Photos live on work orders and on jobs. There is no inspection checklist, no condition report, and no comparison between move-in and move-out.

## Accounting

There is no ledger. The awarded work-order price is a single number on that work order. Rent charges, expenses, owner statements, and late fees are not tracked.

## Reviews

A contractor is chosen from their [portfolio](contractor-directory.md) and their bid, not from a rating. There is no star rating, no written review, and no completed-job score.

## Staff and teams

Properties belong to a single landlord user. There is no property manager, leasing agent, or staff seat under that account.

## Stronger sign-in

Password reset works. A new account can sign in immediately. Email confirmation is not required. Failed logins do not lock the account. There is no second factor and no third-party sign-in.
