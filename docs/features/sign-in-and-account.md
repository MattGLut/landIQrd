# Sign in and account

**Status:** Ready. Landlord, contractor, tenant, and admin.

The front door is the wordmark on a cream page. There is no marketing site in front of it.

![Log in](../walkthrough/01-login.png)

1. Sign in, create an account, or reset a password. Sign-up offers tenant, landlord, or contractor. A public signup stays inactive until the person opens the confirmation email. **Didn't receive confirmation instructions?** on the sign-in page sends that email again.
2. An invite link forces the tenant role and confirms the email, because the invite itself was sent to that inbox. Admins are created in the admin console, already confirmed, not on the public form.
3. Account holds a preferred name and avatar, a light or dark theme for this device, notification toggles, and email and password.

Sign in as `landlord@propman.test`, then open the name in the header and choose the account page. Profile, Notifications, and Email & security sit in the left column of that page.

![Account profile](images/account-profile.png)

Legal name and email are shown and are not edited on this form. Appearance is Light, Dark, or System.
